## QA Summary

Ran 18 scenarios against the running dev app. They cover:

- the three endpoints (`GET /plans`, `/me/entitlements`, `/me/subscription`), called unauthenticated and as a newly registered person;
- the operator actions (grant, extend and revoke through `OperatorSubscriptions`);
- the `billing:lapse-subscriptions` command, with subscription dates moved into the past to force lapses;
- the database effects: subscription rows, `entitlement_changed` audit entries, and data retention after access ends.

All 18 passed. The full gate (`composer test`: lint, Larastan, architecture, Unit, Feature, Contract) is green with 502 tests. No UI is in scope: B3 adds no screens (the operator screens are B12).

The run left some data in the dev database:

- the QA person `qa-1790633153@example.test`;
- their two revoked `manual` subscriptions;
- their audit entries.

The audit entries are append-only by design. The `plus_yearly` plan was retired briefly for TC-016 and set back to `active`; the catalogue was checked again afterwards.
