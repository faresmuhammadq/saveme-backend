## Acceptance Criteria Coverage
All six user stories were exercised either in the browser (US1 claims, US2 accounts and access, US3 plans, US4 audit log, US5 overview, US6 read marks, one operator) or by the automated suites (budget, paging at volume, stale versions, per-channel refusals, second operator).

## Test Suite Results
Baseline before fixes: `XDEBUG_MODE=off dev exec -- composer test`: 52 architecture tests, 3186 passed and 5 skipped (17479 assertions), exit 0. After fixes: 52 architecture tests and 3187 passed, 5 skipped (17486 assertions), exit 0.

## Failures Detail
1. Select primitive never delivered its value to Livewire (`$el` inside an Alpine method is the clicked option). Fixed in `Assets/admin.js`.
2. Grant without a chosen plan answered 500. Fixed in `AccountPage::confirmGrant`; test in `AccessActionsTest`.
3. Account page showed raw lang keys for plan capabilities. Fixed in the view and both language files; assertion added to `AccountPageTest`.
4. Claim queue tabs overflowed the page at 390 px (Arabic). Fixed in `components/tabs.blade.php`.
Notes (not defects): the shell header overflows about 15 px at 390 px in Arabic on every page (also existing pages); Arabic count text "2 مطالبات" uses no plural forms; the unsaved-changes bar appears only after the field loses focus.

## Clean-up
Throwaway operators deactivated. Claims were all closed (one approved, two rejected). The `qa-one` account is pending deletion (30-day grace); `qa-two` is unverified. Plan values were restored to their original values. Dev-database rows for the QA people, claims and the manual subscription were left in place because the audit log is append-only; Mailpit mails remain.
