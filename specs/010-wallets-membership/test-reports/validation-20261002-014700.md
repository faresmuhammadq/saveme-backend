## Observational Notes

### US2 — Scenario 5 (currency)

- ⚠ The refusal of a currency change on a wallet that has postings cannot be reached over HTTP until B6 (no postings exist; `PostingTotals` is the provisional `NoPostingsYet`). Over HTTP only the unlocked path and `currency_locked: false` were observed; the locked path is covered by `tests/Unit/Wallets/WalletRulesTest.php` and `tests/Contract/Wallets/UpsertWalletTest.php`.

### US4 — Scenarios 3 to 6 (transfer)

- ⚠ Before B9 a wallet can only have its owner, so the editor (B) and viewer (C) were inserted directly into `wallet_members` under the wallet lock, taking `change_seq` values from the wallet counter (see `test-reports/http/seed.txt`).
- ⚠ US4 Scenario 6 was resent by the **previous** owner (now an editor) — the realistic retry after a lost response — and returned 200 unchanged. The contract says this replay is answered "to any active member"; this is ahead of FORBIDDEN in the order, which differs from the generic precedence in spec §6 (recorded as an assumed default in `summary.md`).

### All scenarios

- Every success body carried `meta: {}` (object), never `[]`.
- No response exceeded 1 s on the local stack.
