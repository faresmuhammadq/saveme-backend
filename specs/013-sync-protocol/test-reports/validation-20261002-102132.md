>
> - **How it ran.** Every acceptance scenario was executed end to end over HTTP with Python `requests` (curl-equivalent) against `http://saveme-backend.test/api/v1`.
> - **Who ran it.** The run used fresh people `qa013-{a,b,c,u}-e2…` seeded through `UserFactory` and signed in through `POST /auth/login`, and fresh wallets ("E2E …"), independent of the QA run.
> - **Seeding by hand.** Memberships were seeded under the wallet counter and the member's user counter, because invitations arrive in B9. Tombstones were aged in `dev tinker`, and `dev sync:purge-tombstones` ran the retention job.
> - **Evidence.** Raw requests and responses are in `test-reports/http/<story>-<scenario>.txt`, and the seed is in `e2e-seed.txt`. Screenshots do not apply.

## Failure Details

None. A first run reported US2 — Scenario 6 as failed because the harness read the balance before the twin per-record transaction was created. This was a harness error, and the corrected rerun above passes. No product change was needed.

## Observational Notes

### US1 — Scenario 6, US3 — Scenario 4 (concurrency)
- Writers and the paging reader ran as parallel HTTP clients. This is real concurrency through PHP-FPM.
- 50 wallet writes, and 15 category saves plus 5 sign-in/sign-out cycles, were all seen. None were skipped.

### US4 — Scenario 5
- Wallet deletion leaves a transfer into the deleted wallet live (ADR-0015 rule 5).
- That live transfer keeps the tombstoned wallet through the purge.

### Performance (all scenarios)
- ⚠ Slow responses were observed on the dev web stack: a 50-item batch takes about 12 s, and a single write about 0.8 s.
- The cause is Xdebug `develop` mode loaded in the dev runtime. SC-006 was measured with it off (plan.md T046).
