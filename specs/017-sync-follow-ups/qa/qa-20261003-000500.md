## QA Summary

**Setup**
- Ran `dev queue:restart`. The supervisor in the `saveme-backend-main` container runs `queue:work` (database queue) and `schedule:work`, so the `ResendMemberNames` listener and `RestampMemberNames` jobs ran on the real worker.
- No migration in this spec. The dev database was not reset, seeded or put into maintenance mode.
- People were registered through `POST /auth/register` (so they have profiles), verified with the token from the Mailpit mail, and signed in through `POST /auth/login`. Addresses were `qs017-<role>-<run>@example.test`. One person stayed unverified.
- The owner got Plus through `OperatorSubscriptions::grant`, so she could share. Every membership came from a link invitation and `POST /invitations/accept`. One partner left through `POST /wallets/{id}/leave`. One wallet was archived and one deleted through the API.
- Purge horizon: the QA wallet's (or the QA user's) tombstones had `deleted_at` moved 91 days into the past. Then `dev sync:purge-tombstones` ran.

**Result**
- 202 checks in 13 groups (TC-000 to TC-012). Every check passes in its final form.
- `sync.yaml` (v1.10) was not changed.

**Clean-up**: every QA and E2E person was deleted afterwards, with their wallets, memberships, invitations, transactions, rules, occurrences, category references, categories, activity entries, subscriptions and sessions (`storage/app/private/qa017/cleanup.php`, not committed). The Mailpit messages to `qs017*` addresses were deleted too. Audit entries stay, because the table is append-only by design. While setting up E2E, the register throttle counter for the QA origin was cleared. That counter held only the QA registrations.

**Evidence**: `qa/responses/TC-*.txt`. Credentials and invitation or verification tokens are redacted.

## Failure Details

No product defect.

### Test-side corrections (no product defect)

- **TC-006, first run**: the "no audit / no activity entry" checks queried columns that do not exist (`created_at`, where the real columns are `occurred_at`). A direct look at the rows showed nothing in the rename window. The script now uses `occurred_at` over the window from the rename to the queue going idle. It passes.
- **E2E set-up**: `POST /auth/register` is throttled at 10 per hour per origin. QA had used all 10, so the QA origin's counter (only QA registrations) was cleared before E2E.

### Observations (not defects)

- A heads cursor is not bound to its reader, the same as every other collection cursor. Its payload is `{wallet_id}`, signed. Replaying another person's cursor only moves the start point. Results are still filtered to the caller's own memberships, so nothing leaks.
- `sync:purge-tombstones` runs over the whole dev database. Only QA rows had been aged.
