## Fixes

- `PlayLog::reconciled` logged its pass name under the key `pass`, which the secret scrubber matched (`pass(word)?`), so every reconciliation line read `"pass":"[REDACTED]"`. Renamed to `run`.

## Notes

- The fake script file recomputes `expires_in_days` on every read, so a purchase re-read in a loop looks like a renewal each time (extra `renew` audit rows on dev); not a product defect.
- An unknown `kid` in a push token makes the verifier refresh Google's public certificate endpoint (one GET to googleapis.com certs, by design, at most once a minute). I hit it once on dev and re-seeded the cache.
- Workers must listen to the `billing` queue; dev ran `queue:work --queue=billing,notifications,default --stop-when-empty`.
- Cleanup: `.env` restored byte for byte, `storage/qa024` removed, the dev `plus_monthly` plan's temporary `google_play` price removed, throwaway users and their Play rows deleted; audit rows left.
