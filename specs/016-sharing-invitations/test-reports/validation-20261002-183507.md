**Clean-up**: all E2E data was deleted afterwards together with the QA data, including the Mailpit messages.

## Failure Details

None in the final run. The first runs failed in the script only:
- US4-1: the script expected two mails, but the superseded invitation was closed before its mail job ran, and a closed invitation sends nothing (correct). The script now waits for the first mail before re-inviting.
- US6-3: platform categories have no Category Reference. The leaver now uses their own category.
- US7-1: the audit check looked for the wrong subject. The demotion audit entries have `subject_type: wallet`.

## Observational Notes

### Owner manages pending invitations — Scenario 5

- ⚠ Until the hourly sweep runs, an invitation past its expiry instant is still listed as `pending` and is still on the feed as pending. The frozen contract documents this, and a client should treat `expires_at` < now as expired.

### Invitee declines, or the invitation is not theirs — Scenario 1

- ⚠ When a pending email invitation is superseded before the worker sends its mail, that mail is never sent. This is intended: closed invitations send nothing.
