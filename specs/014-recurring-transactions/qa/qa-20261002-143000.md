## Failure Details

### TC-090: Changing the amount of a total or gam'eya rule after a resolved round

**Status**: ❌ FAIL, then fixed and ✅ on retest.

**Steps to Reproduce**
1. Create an installment rule: `amount_basis: total`, 1,000,000 over 12 rounds from 2026-08-05.
2. Confirm round 1 (83,334).
3. Save the rule with `amount_minor: 1200000`.

**Expected**: the shares of a total always sum exactly to the total (P-03 rule 4, SC-006). FR-007 already freezes `occurrence_count` for these rules "because it fixes every round's share and the payout".

**Actual**:
- The save was 200 and only the pending rounds were re-split to 100,000. The series now summed to 83,334 + 11 × 100,000 = 1,183,334, not 1,200,000.
- The same applied to a gam'eya: after a skipped round, raising the contribution from 1,000 to 2,000 recomputed the payout as 5 × 2,000 = 10,000, while the rounds were 1,000 + 4 × 2,000.

**Evidence**: [TC-090](responses/TC-090.txt) (first block: before the fix; "RETEST after fix": after).

**Severity**: High (money totals).

**Fix**
- `RecurringRuleRules`: for a gam'eya or `total` rule, `amount_minor` joins `occurrence_count` among the members frozen once any occurrence is resolved (`IMMUTABLE_FIELD /amount_minor`).
- New unit test in `tests/Unit/Wallets/Recurring/RuleRulesTest.php`.
- FR-007, data-model, plan A-37, the contract summary and the `upsertRecurringRule` description in `recurring.yaml` were amended. The change is description only: no schema, code or status changes.

### Test-side corrections (no product defect)

Each first-run failure was traced to a wrong expectation in the QA script, checked against the contract, then corrected and rerun.

1. **TC-010**: a stale version with an *identical* body is a replay (200). Precedence puts the replay before the stale check. Retested with a changing body, which gives 409.
2. **TC-014 (4 checks)**: the occurrence had been bumped by the template edit, so both racers held a stale version and both got 409. Retested with the current version: one 200 and one 409.
3. **TC-050**: the gam'eya payout (round 4, 2027-01-05) lies beyond the horizon, so it is not materialised yet. It appears together with its round (verified in TC-052, round 2, and in the E2E US4-1).
4. **TC-051**: `payout_round > occurrence_count` is OUT_OF_RANGE at `/occurrence_count` ("a count below `payout_round`"). `total` without a count is INVALID_ENUM_VALUE at `/amount_basis`. Both are as `recurring.yaml` documents.
5. **TC-080**: `between:` is not an operator of the shared grammar, so the response is INVALID_QUERY_PARAMETER, the same as on transactions. A known operator on the wrong field (`in:` on `date`) gives FILTER_OPERATOR_NOT_SUPPORTED.
